# Nimbus Robotics — Company Overview

## About Nimbus Robotics

Nimbus Robotics builds autonomous ground delivery robots for the last mile.
Founded in **2019** in Portland, Oregon by **Maya Chen** and **David Okafor**,
the company deploys fleets of sidewalk robots for retailers, grocery chains, and
logistics providers. In **2023**, Nimbus moved its headquarters to
**Austin, Texas**, where its 24/7 network operations center (NOC) is also
located.

## Mission

Make the last mile of delivery faster, cheaper, and lower-carbon than
van-based delivery, one autonomous robot at a time.

## Funding

- **Series C — June 2024**: $240M, led by **Meridian Capital**. Post-money
  valuation of **$1.8B**.
- Prior rounds: Pre-seed ($4M, 2019), Series A ($28M, 2020), Series B
  ($95M, 2022).

## Growth

- **2020** — First commercial deployment; NB-1 Courier launched September 2020.
- **2022** — 10,000th robot delivered.
- **2024** — NB-2 Hauler launched April 2024; fleet passed 12,000 robots.
- **2025 (Q1)** — 12,400 robots operating across 43 cities.
