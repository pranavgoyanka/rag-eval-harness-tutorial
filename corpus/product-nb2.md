# NB-2 "Hauler" — Product Specifications

The **NB-2 Hauler** is Nimbus's heavy-duty delivery robot, announced in
**November 2023** and commercially launched in **April 2024**. It targets
larger payloads (grocery, bulk goods, B2B routes) and harsher climates.

## General specifications

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Payload capacity | 60 kg |
| Top speed | 30 km/h |
| Range (single charge) | 110 km |
| Battery | 72 V lithium-ion, 18.5 kWh (swappable pack) |
| Weight (unloaded) | 142 kg |
| Dimensions (L × W × H) | 150 × 100 × 120 cm |
| Ingress protection | IP67 |
| Operating temperature | −20 °C to 50 °C |
| Mean time between failures (MTBF) | 15,000 hours |
| Fleet uptime (2024) | 99.4% |
| Average deliveries per robot per day | 52 |
| List price | $32,000 per unit |

## Battery and charging

The NB-2 uses a **swappable battery pack**:

| Action | Time |
|---|---|
| Standard charge (0–100%) | 3 h |
| Fast charge (0–80%) | 1 h |
| Pack swap | under 90 seconds |

A pack is replaced after 1,000 charge cycles.

## Sensor suite

- 2 × solid-state LiDAR units
- 10 × RGB cameras (360° coverage)
- 16 × ultrasonic proximity sensors
- 1 × GNSS/GPS receiver with RTK correction
- Pedestrian detection range: 45 m

## Reliability notes

- Average remote intervention rate: 3.1 per 1,000 km driven.
- Scheduled maintenance every 800 operating hours.
- Traction control allows operation in light snow; the NB-2 is rated to
  −20 °C.
