# Safety Protocols

## Emergency stop

- Every robot has a physical **emergency stop (e-stop) button** on the rear
  panel.
- Pressing e-stop cuts drive power within **100 ms** and latches until an
  operator resets it.
- Auto-braking: from 24 km/h the robot comes to a full stop within **8 m**.

## Incident classification and reporting

| Level | Definition | Reporting deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | No injury, no property damage | within 24 hours |
| Level 2 | Minor injury (first-aid level) | within 4 hours |
| Level 3 | Serious injury or major property damage | within 1 hour, and notify the regional manager |

All incidents are logged in the Nimbus incident system and reviewed by the
safety team within 5 business days.

## Fleet safety statistics (2024)

- Collision rate: **0.03 per 10,000 km** (fleet-wide)
- Fleet downtime: 0.8%
- Liability insurance: $10M per incident

## Operator certification

- Remote operators complete **16 hours** of initial training before working
  solo shifts.
- Annual recertification: **8 hours**, due every 12 months from the initial
  certification date.

## Weather and environment

- Robots continue operating in rain (IP65/IP67); in **heavy rain** their
  speed is reduced by **20%**.
- **NB-1 is suspended in snow**; the NB-2 can operate in light snow down to
  −20 °C with traction control.
