Covers software, OTA updates, 24/7 NOC monitoring, and standard remote
support.

| Model | Monthly |
|---|---|
| NB-1 | $450 |

## Volume discounts (hardware)

| Units in a single order | Discount |
|---|---|
| 100+ | 8% |
| 250+ | 12% |
| 500+ | 18% |

Discounts apply to hardware only, not to subscription fees.

## Contract terms

- Minimum contract length: **24 months**.
- Early termination fee: **3 months of subscription fees** for the affected
  robots.
- Site deployment fee: **$2,500 per site** (one-time).
- Financing: 36-month lease available at 9.5% APR.

## Pilot program

- Duration: 30 days.
- Fleet size: up to 5 robots.
- Fee: $500 (non-refundable).
- Pilot robots are NB-1 units unless the customer's payloads exceed 25 kg,
  in which case NB-2 units may be used.
