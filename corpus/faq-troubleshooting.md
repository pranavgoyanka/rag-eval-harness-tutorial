# FAQ and Troubleshooting

## Error codes

| Code | Meaning | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| E101 | LiDAR obstruction | Power down and clean the sensor array |
| E204 | Battery below 5% | Robot auto-returns to its dock to charge |
| E317 | Geofence breach | Robot halts; operator approval is required to resume |
| E402 | Over-temperature (internal 55 °C) | Robot pauses; resumes after 15 min of cooling |

## Common issues

**My robot is stuck.**
The robot attempts to free itself up to 3 times, then pages the NOC for
remote assistance.

**The customer app went offline.**
Robots continue operating from cached routes; deliveries already in progress
complete normally.

**Is support available at night?**
Yes. support@nimbusrobotics.com and the status page
(status.nimbusrobotics.com) are monitored 24/7 for critical issues. Onsite
response: critical 4 business hours, non-critical 24 hours (see Fleet
Operations).
