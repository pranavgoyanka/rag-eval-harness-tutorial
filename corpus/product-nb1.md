# NB-1 "Courier" — Product Specifications

The **NB-1 Courier** is Nimbus's first-generation delivery robot. Announced in
**March 2020** and commercially launched in **September 2020**, it is designed
for lightweight, high-frequency last-mile deliveries (parcels, groceries,
retail).

## General specifications

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Payload capacity | 25 kg |
| Top speed | 24 km/h |
| Range (single charge) | 80 km |
| Battery | 48 V lithium-ion, 10.2 kWh |
| Weight (unloaded) | 85 kg |
| Dimensions (L × W × H) | 120 × 85 × 95 cm |
| Ingress protection | IP65 |
| Operating temperature | −10 °C to 45 °C |
| Mean time between failures (MTBF) | 12,000 hours |
| Fleet uptime (2024) | 99.2% |
| Average deliveries per robot per day | 38 |
| List price | $18,500 per unit |

## Charging

| Mode | Time |
|---|---|
| Standard charge (0–100%) | 2 h 15 min |
| Fast charge (0–80%) | 45 min |

Robots dock and charge during the nightly service window (01:00–05:00 local
time) unless on an active delivery run.

## Sensor suite

- 2 × solid-state LiDAR units
- 8 × RGB cameras (360° coverage)
- 12 × ultrasonic proximity sensors
- 1 × GNSS/GPS receiver with RTK correction
- Pedestrian detection range: 40 m

## Reliability notes

- Average remote intervention rate: 4.2 per 1,000 km driven.
- Battery should be replaced after 800 charge cycles (when capacity falls
  below 80% of rated).
- Scheduled maintenance every 500 operating hours or 2,000 km, whichever
  comes first.
